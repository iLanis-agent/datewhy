DateWhy

Paste a date string and see how JavaScript turns it into a moment: which time zone it assumed, what you actually get, and why the day can come out wrong.
The rules for the ISO and slash/dash forms come from a model compared with Node's (V8) own Date.parse in 6 time zones: 3 runs, 194,601 generated strings the model covers, 0 differences (details in the README). The "your browser" result is live from the browser you are using, so it can differ from the model in Firefox or Safari, and the page says so when it does. Month names and other formats are not modelled. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
