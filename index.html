Open the app
new Date("2026-03-01") midnight UTC new Date("2026-03-01T00:00") midnight local in Los Angeles the first one is Feb 28, 4pm

What it does

  • Reads a date string the way Chrome and Node do and says which zone it assumed: UTC, your local time, or the one in the string.
  • Shows the exact moment in UTC and in any zone you pick, and warns when the calendar day shifts.
  • Explains the odd cases: unpadded months, Feb 30 rolling into March, 24:00, offsets like +05 that are rejected.
  • Compares the live result of your own browser with the model, to catch engines that disagree.

How far to trust it

  • The model matched Node's own date parser on 194,601 generated strings in 6 time zones with 0 differences. Month names and other free-form formats are not modelled, and Firefox and Safari were not tested.
